---
name: Oracle facial animation
description: Visual and timing constraints for keeping the Oracle chat portrait believable.
---

Use matched closed-mouth and slightly open-mouth portrait states with direct eye contact. Do not draw a geometric mouth overlay or use a fixed repeating interval. Mouth movements should be short, irregular, and active only while response characters are appearing.

**Why:** A synthetic overlay and evenly timed frame switching looked mechanical and obscured the face. The desired result is a restrained human speaking motion with a small glimpse of teeth.

**How to apply:** Preserve the same crop, identity, lighting, and gaze across face states. Keep the opening small, vary open and closed durations, and stop immediately after the typewriter finishes.
